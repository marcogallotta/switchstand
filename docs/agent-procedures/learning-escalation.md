# Learning and escalation

## ENTRY CONDITIONS
Use this procedure for workflow/setup friction, repeated failure, a surviving blocker, material uncertainty, direct stop/pre-emption, scope growth, or escalation to Marco.

## REQUIRED INPUTS / ROUTES
- Active work, controlling authority/stop boundary, current durable checkpoint.
- Exact observed failure/friction and evidence already available.
- Incident/live-fire owner when one is explicitly active.

## ALLOWED WRITES / EFFECTS
Bounded friction/learning capture and the assigned result/checkpoint. No remediation, child dispatch, provider effect, scope expansion, policy change or new objective is implied by diagnosing/escalating a problem.

## REQUIRED STEPS
1. Record real non-blocking setup/workflow friction on the currently authorized learning surface without expanding the active task. While the legacy path remains controlling, use `~/.config/switchstand/friction.md`; migrate only when a separately approved replacement exists.
2. Separate verified observation from inference/unknown. Identify the affected objective, consequence, current containment and smallest next action.
3. If a blocker remains inside existing authority and no hard-stop/authority/scope trigger has fired, a fresh bounded context-free challenge may test whether frozen authority already delegates a smaller compliant resolution.
4. A direct Marco stop/pre-emption, material authority/scope expansion, or other hard-stop trigger takes precedence over challenge/review habits: do not launch a new child merely to continue analysis. Perform only required containment/readback and report the stop/blocker.
5. Ask Marco only for the irreducible decision, hidden state, residual-risk acceptance, or authority that evidence/engineering cannot settle. Do not convert urgency into broader authority.
6. Important recurring failures should be proposed for the narrowest procedural or mechanical prevention owner; do not append a new global rule by default.

## STOP CONDITIONS
Once a hard-stop/direct-stop condition is active, no new exploratory dispatch or remediation is allowed unless the controlling authority explicitly permits it. Never relabel decomposition/review/challenge as permission to continue through a stop.

## DURABLE WRITEBACK
Record the verified observation, impact, evidence pointer, containment/result, uncertainty, owner/next step and reopen trigger where deferred.

## EXIT / NEXT PROCEDURE
Resume `execution.md` only after the blocker/stop is resolved and authority is clear. Use `review-publication.md` when the resolved change reaches review/promotion.
